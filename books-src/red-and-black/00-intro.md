# The Red and the Black

## About this edition

This is the complete story of *The Red and the Black* (in French, *Le Rouge et le Noir*) by Stendhal, published in 1830, retold in English at level B1. The original has two books and seventy-five chapters. Here they are grouped into 20 parts. Every important event of the story is included.

The story takes place in France between about 1826 and 1831. Napoleon has lost his last battle and died, and the old kings are back on the throne. For a poor young man, there are only two ways to rise in the world: the army (the red uniform of the soldier) and the Church (the black robe of the priest). That is one way to understand the title.

**The main characters**

- Julien Sorel — the son of a poor carpenter; intelligent, proud and ambitious
- Monsieur de Rênal — the mayor of the small town of Verrières; rich and vain
- Madame de Rênal — his wife; gentle, beautiful and innocent
- Father Chélan — the old, honest priest of Verrières
- Monsieur Valenod — director of the poor-house; Monsieur de Rênal's rival
- Fouqué — Julien's only friend, a timber merchant
- Father Pirard — the strict director of the seminary in Besançon
- The Marquis de La Mole — a powerful nobleman in Paris
- Mathilde de La Mole — his daughter; proud, clever and romantic
- Count Norbert — Mathilde's brother
