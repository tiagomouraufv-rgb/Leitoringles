## Part 10 — The Ladder

On his way to Paris, Julien could not resist. He went back to Verrières to see Madame de Rênal one last time. It had been fourteen months since he had left.

He arrived at night and walked to the house. Everything was dark. He found a long ladder in the garden, which the gardener used for the fruit trees. He carried it to the wall under her window and climbed up. His heart was beating fast. He knocked very softly on the window.

For a long time, nothing happened. Then the window opened a little. Madame de Rênal looked out. When she saw him, she was terrified.

"Go away!" she whispered. "Go away at once! I have repented. God has forgiven me. I will never see you again."

During the fourteen months, she had suffered terribly. She had believed that her love was a great sin, and she had confessed it to a priest. She had prayed and fasted, and she had promised God that she would never see Julien again. She had tried to forget him. She had become very religious.

But Julien did not go away. He climbed into the room. He talked to her for a long time in the dark. He told her about the seminary, about his loneliness, about his suffering. She listened in silence. Then he told her that he was going to Paris, and that he would probably never see her again. She began to cry.

At last, in spite of all her promises, she could not resist him. She threw herself into his arms. All her religious fears disappeared in a moment of happiness. She loved him more than ever.

**Hidden in the Room**

When the morning came, Julien could not leave. There were servants everywhere in the garden. Madame de Rênal hid him in her room all day. She brought him food that she had taken secretly from the kitchen, and they laughed like children at the danger. She was so happy that she forgot everything.

In the afternoon, her husband came to her door and knocked. "Open the door! I want to speak to you!"

Madame de Rênal hid Julien under a pile of clothes in a corner of the room. Then she opened the door. Her husband came in and talked about some business. He walked around the room. Julien, under the clothes, held his breath. Monsieur de Rênal did not see anything. At last he left.

**The Escape**

That night, Julien prepared to leave. But just as he was climbing down the ladder, a servant saw him in the moonlight and gave the alarm.

"Thieves! Thieves!" he shouted.

Julien jumped down and ran across the garden. Shots were fired behind him. A bullet passed close to his head. He ran faster, climbed over the wall, and escaped into the night.

When he was far away, and safe, he stopped to catch his breath. He looked back at the dark house. He knew he would never see her again. "Goodbye," he whispered. Then he took the road to Paris.

Behind him, Madame de Rênal stood at her window, trembling. She had heard the shots. She did not know if he was alive or dead.


*Here ends the first book of the story.*
