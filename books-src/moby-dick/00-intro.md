# Moby Dick

## About this edition

This is the complete story of *Moby Dick* by Herman Melville (1851), retold in English at level B1. The original has 135 chapters and an epilogue. Here they are grouped into 33 parts. Every event of the story is included. The chapters where Melville stops the story to write about whales, ships and whaling are shortened into a few paragraphs, so that you can still learn what he wanted to tell you.

The story is told by a young sailor called Ishmael. Tap any word to see its meaning. Tap a marked phrase to see a grammar note.

**The main characters**

- Ishmael — a young American who goes to sea on a whaling ship; he tells the story
- Queequeg — a harpooner from an island in the South Pacific; Ishmael's best friend
- Captain Ahab — the captain of the Pequod; he lost a leg to the white whale
- Starbuck — the first mate; serious, brave and religious
- Stubb — the second mate; always calm and smoking his pipe
- Flask — the third mate; short and aggressive
- Tashtego — a Native American harpooner, from Gay Head
- Daggoo — an enormous African harpooner
- Fedallah — a mysterious man from Asia, Ahab's secret harpooner
- Pip — a young African-American cabin boy
- Moby Dick — a huge white sperm whale, famous among all whalemen

**The ship**

The Pequod is an old whaling ship from Nantucket, an island near the coast of Massachusetts, in the United States. In the 1840s, American ships sailed all over the world for three or four years at a time to hunt sperm whales. The men cut the fat from the whales and boiled it on the ship to make oil, which people used in their lamps.
