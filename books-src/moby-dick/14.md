## Part 14 — The Town-Ho's Story

Not long after the *Albatross*, we met another whaling ship, the *Town-Ho*. This time there was a gam, and the crew of the *Town-Ho* gave us news of the white whale. They also told a strange and terrible story. Ahab never heard it, but our sailors did. Later, I told the story to some Spanish friends in Lima, in a hotel called the Golden Inn. This is how I told it.

"Gentlemen," I said, "some years ago, the *Town-Ho* was sailing in the Pacific Ocean, near the equator. One day, the sailors discovered that the ship was leaking. Water was coming in through a hole somewhere below. They had to work at the pumps every day to keep the ship from sinking. The captain decided to sail to the nearest port to repair her.

"On board the *Town-Ho* there was a sailor called Steelkilt. He came from the Great Lakes, in the north of America. He was a tall, strong, proud man, and the other sailors admired him. Everyone liked him, except one man: the mate, Radney.

"Radney was an ugly, rough man from Martha's Vineyard. He was jealous of Steelkilt, because Steelkilt was handsome and popular. Radney hated him and wanted to humiliate him.

"One evening, after Steelkilt had worked very hard at the pumps, Radney ordered him to take a broom and sweep the deck, and then to clean up the dirt left by the pigs that ran loose on board. This was a job for a boy, not for a strong sailor, and everyone knew that Radney wanted to insult him.

"Steelkilt refused politely. 'That is not my job,' he said. 'There are boys on the ship for that.'

"Radney was furious. He took a heavy hammer and came close to Steelkilt, raising it over his head. 'Do it!' he shouted.

"Steelkilt stepped back slowly, and Radney followed him. Steelkilt warned him: 'If you touch me with that hammer, I'll kill you.' But Radney did not listen. He touched Steelkilt's cheek with the hammer. At once, Steelkilt hit him in the face, and broke his jaw. Radney fell to the deck, bleeding like a whale.

"Then there was a mutiny. Steelkilt and some of his friends fought against the officers and locked themselves in the front of the ship. The captain came with a gun and told them to come out and go back to work. They refused unless the captain promised not to punish Steelkilt. The captain did not promise. So he locked them in below, without food or water.

"After some days, hungry and thirsty, most of the men gave up and came out. In the end, only Steelkilt and two others were left. Then the two others betrayed Steelkilt. They tied him up and handed him over to the captain. The captain tied all three of them to the ropes and whipped the two traitors. But when he was going to whip Steelkilt, Steelkilt whispered something to him, and the captain stepped back, frightened. He did not touch him.

"Then Radney, with his broken jaw, came up and took the whip. He whipped Steelkilt himself, very hard, while Steelkilt said nothing.

"When he was free, Steelkilt decided to kill Radney. He made a secret plan. Every night, Radney had to stand on watch alone. Steelkilt would wait until he was asleep, and then he would kill him with a heavy iron ball tied in a bag.

"But, gentlemen, before he could do it, God did it for him.

"The very next day, at dawn, the man at the mast-head shouted that he had seen a whale. It was a huge white whale — Moby Dick! The boats were lowered, and Radney, in the front of his boat, threw his harpoon into the whale. At that moment, the whale rose up under the boat, and Radney was thrown into the sea, right in front of the whale's head. The great white whale opened his crooked jaw, took Radney in his teeth, and went down with him into the deep. Radney was never seen again.

"When the *Town-Ho* reached land at last, Steelkilt and most of the crew ran away. The captain had to find new sailors to take the ship home. I have heard that Steelkilt is still alive somewhere, and that he still sails."

When I finished, my Spanish friends asked me if the story was really true.

"Gentlemen," I said, "I swear it on the Bible. I have met men who sailed on the *Town-Ho*. I have spoken to Steelkilt's friends. It is all true."
