## Part 18 — The Monkey-Rope and Two Heads

**The Monkey-Rope**

During the cutting in, someone had to go down onto the whale's body in the water, to put the great hook into the blubber. This was the harpooner's job. Queequeg was Starbuck's harpooner, so he had to do it. He stood on the slippery back of the dead whale, half in the water, with sharks all around him.

To keep him safe, he was tied to a long rope, and I was tied to the other end of it on the ship. This rope is called the **monkey-rope**. My job was to hold it and pull him up if he fell or if the sharks came too close. But the rope was tied round both our bodies. So if Queequeg fell into the sea and sank, I would be pulled in after him, and we would both drown.

I understood then that I was not completely free. My life depended on another man's. If he made a mistake, I would die for it. I was like his Siamese twin. And then I thought: every man in the world is in the same situation. We are all tied to other people by invisible monkey-ropes. If your banker fails, you lose your money. If the doctor makes a mistake, you die. We all depend on each other.

Poor Queequeg worked hard, jumping over the whale's back, hitting the sharks away with his spade, while the waves washed over him. When he came back on board, wet and tired, the steward brought him a cup of ginger and water to drink, instead of the brandy he wanted. Stubb was angry. "Ginger! Do you think a cannibal can be warmed up with ginger? Give him a glass of grog!"

**Two Whales**

A few days later, we saw a right whale. Ahab gave orders to kill it, which surprised the sailors, because the *Pequod* usually hunted only sperm whales. Stubb and Flask killed it and brought it to the ship.

"Why does the old man want this ugly right whale?" Flask asked Stubb, as they rowed back.

"Didn't you hear?" said Stubb. "Fedallah told him that a ship with a sperm whale's head on one side and a right whale's head on the other can never be turned over by a storm."

"Do you think that's true?"

"I don't know. But I don't like that Fedallah. Have you noticed that he always hides his tail? I think he keeps it coiled up in his pocket. He's the devil, Flask, I tell you. The devil in disguise."

"Why does Ahab keep him on board, then?"

"I think the old man has made a deal with him. He has sold his soul to him, in exchange for Moby Dick. If I meet that devil one night, I'll pull out his tail, and see what happens."

So now the *Pequod* had two whale heads hanging from her sides: a sperm whale's head on one side, and a right whale's head on the other. With the heavy weight on both sides, she was balanced again. I thought of the philosophers: if you put the head of one philosopher on one side of your mind and the head of another philosopher on the other side, your mind will be balanced too — but heavy!

Meanwhile, I noticed that Fedallah stood looking at the right whale's head, and then at his own hand, as if comparing the lines in them. And when Ahab and Fedallah stood together on the deck, it seemed as if Fedallah's shadow was mixed with Ahab's, and became longer.

**The Two Heads Compared**

Now, while the two heads were hanging there, let us compare them.

The sperm whale's head is noble and grand. It is a huge, smooth, square block, like a great wall. It has a dignity, like an old king. On each side, low down, there is a small eye — much too small for such a huge animal. Because the eyes are on the sides of his head, the sperm whale sees two different pictures at the same time, one on each side, and cannot see straight in front. How can his brain understand two things at once? Perhaps this explains why sperm whales sometimes seem confused when they are attacked by several boats. Its ear is even smaller — a hole so small that you could hardly put a pen in it. And yet a whale hears better than any animal. The lesson is: do not judge things by their size.

The right whale's head is very different. It is ugly and strange, like a giant shoe. Its huge lips look like they could close around a whole boat. Inside its mouth hangs the long, black whalebone, like hair, through which it eats the brit. Its tongue alone gives a great deal of oil. Compared to the sperm whale, the right whale looks like a stupid, ugly animal, while the sperm whale looks like a thinker. The sperm whale is a philosopher, I think, and the right whale is just a hungry stomach.
