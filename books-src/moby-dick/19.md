## Part 19 — Tashtego in the Head

**The Front of the Head**

The front of a sperm whale's head is like a huge wall, completely without bones. It is made of a very tough, elastic material, like the hardest leather. Behind it, there is a great mass of oil and fat. This makes the whole head work like a giant battering-ram. A sperm whale can hit a ship with its head and break a hole in it, as the whale did to the *Essex*. And the whale feels no pain, because there is nothing there to break.

**The Tun**

Inside the top of the head, there is a big empty space, a kind of natural tank, which whalemen call the **case**, or the **tun**, like a huge barrel. It is full of the best and purest oil — the spermaceti. In a big whale, the case can hold about five hundred gallons. When the whale is alive, the spermaceti is liquid and clear. When it meets the cold air, it becomes white and solid, like wax. It makes the finest candles in the world.

**Tashtego Falls In**

To get the spermaceti out, a man must climb onto the top of the whale's head, make a hole in the case, and lower a bucket into it, like a man getting water from a well. Then the full bucket is pulled up, emptied, and lowered again.

Tashtego did this job for our sperm whale's head, which was still hanging over the side of the ship. He went up, made a hole, and began to bring up bucket after bucket of the precious oil, while the men below emptied them. After a long time, the case was almost empty, and Tashtego had to push the bucket deeper and deeper inside.

Suddenly — I don't know how it happened — Tashtego slipped and fell head first into the hole! The thick oil closed over him, and he disappeared inside the whale's head.

"Man overboard!" cried Daggoo, who was the first to see it. He climbed up onto the head to help. But at that moment, there was a terrible noise. One of the great hooks that held the head had broken. The huge head swung for a moment, then fell into the sea and began to sink — with Tashtego inside it!

Everyone screamed. Daggoo jumped back onto the ship just in time. The head went down slowly into the deep water, carrying poor Tashtego with it, like a coffin.

But before anyone could do anything, a naked figure jumped from the side of the ship into the sea, with a sword in his hand. It was Queequeg! He dived down after the head and disappeared.

The men watched the water in silence. A minute passed. Another minute. Nothing. "He's lost too," someone whispered.

Then, suddenly, an arm came out of the water, and then a head — Queequeg's head! And with his other arm he was pulling a second man by his long black hair. It was Tashtego! Queequeg had dived down to the sinking head, cut a hole in its side with his sword, put his arm inside and pulled Tashtego out, head first, like a midwife bringing a baby into the world.

They were pulled on board. Tashtego was half dead, but he soon recovered. Queequeg was a hero again. And I thought: what a strange death that would have been — to drown in the sweetest, most precious oil in the world!

**The Whale's Face**

A sperm whale has no real face. It has no nose. Its eyes and ears are on the sides of its head, far away from each other. When you look at the front of its head, you see only a great empty wall, with deep wrinkles across it, like a mountain landscape or a wide prairie. And yet this face has a great dignity. It looks like the face of a god. It seems to say: "I am beyond your understanding." The more I study it, the less I understand it. How can I explain the whale's face, when I cannot even explain the face of a man?

**The Brain**

A sperm whale's head is enormous, but its brain is very small — not bigger than a man's. It is hidden deep inside the head, behind the case, like a nut inside a thick shell. You could go twenty feet into the head without finding it. But perhaps the whale's real strength is not in its brain, but in its long spine. A strong spine makes a strong character. And the sperm whale has the strongest spine in the world. I wish more men had a spine like that.
